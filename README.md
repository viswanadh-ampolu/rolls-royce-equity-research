## Key findings
- **Services-led model:** about 69% of Civil Aerospace revenue (£7.2bn of £10.4bn) comes from aftermarket services, mostly long-term service agreements paid per engine flying hour.
- **Margin step-up:** group underlying operating margin rose from 19.1% (H1 2025) to 22.5% (H1 2026), and operating profit grew 46% to £2.5bn.
- **Guidance raised:** FY2026 underlying operating profit is now guided at £4.7–4.9bn, with free cash flow of £3.8–4.0bn.
- **Valuation:** EV/EBIT is about 25x, compared with a 5-year average of 20.4x.
- **Upside options:** data-centre demand in Power Systems and the SMR (small modular reactor) programme.
